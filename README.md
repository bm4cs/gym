# Gym programs

## Full body

These hit all major muscle groups every workout.

- [Full body 2018](./full-body-2018.md) an advanced program with tons of volume per workout, must be coupled with adaquate recovery
- [Full body 2021](./full-body-2021.md) a remix of the 2018 edition with some minor variations

## Splits

These target categories of muscle groups, allowing the other parts of the body to recover.

- [Skeetsy's aesthetic program](./aesthetic-01.md) focused splits and bigger rep ranges for shredding
- [Max's challenge advanced program](./maxs-challenge-advanced.md) rolls through leg, push and pull days, however you please
- [Old school split program](./old-school-split.md) hypertrophy vibes with pyramids throughout
- [Ken Win's 2024 powerlifting program](./ken-win-2024-split.md) program designed for 5 workouts per week by a world champion powerlifter, front loads the big lifts into the start of the week
