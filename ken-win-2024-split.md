# Ken Win 2024 powerlifting split

Well balanced program focused on building strength and is interlaced with recovery targeting smaller muscle groups on the easier going days. Noteably an arm and abs exercise to end each workout.

## Monday - legs

- back squats (6x6)
- lying leg curl
- hip abductor
- dumbbell goblet squat
- leg extensions
- seated incline bicep curl
- cable bicep curl
- barbell bicep curl
- captains chair leg raises

## Tuesday - chest

- barbell bench press
- barbell include bench press
- pendlay row
- dumbbell fly
- cable fly
- floor press narrow grip
- weighted dips
- russian twist

## Wednesday - back

- deadlift
- barbell hip thrust
- seated cable row
- standing cable pull down
- lat pulldown
- planks

# Thursday - legs and chest

- barbell bench press narrow
- dumbbell close grip chest press
- decline barbell bench press
- pec deck
- chest press machine
- explosive push-ups
- bulgarian split squats
- pin squats
- walking lunges
- hammer curls
- decline sit-ups

# Friday - back and shoulders

- paused deadlift
- romanian deadlift
- pendlay rows
- dumbbell bent-over fly
- overhead press
- dumbbell front raises
- cable front raise
- cable side raise
- overhead cable tricep extension
- seated tricep extension
- close grip floor press
- mountain climber
